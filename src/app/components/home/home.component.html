Before you come

What a first visit looks like

No dress code and no sign-up. Find a seat, sing if you want to, and someone will say hello afterwards.

  • Sundays at 10:30 and 17:00

    Midweek service on Wednesday at 19:00. Services run about ninety minutes.

  • 5 Sunbury Avenue, Auckland Park

    Street parking outside the sanctuary. We are a short drive from Melville.

  • Bring your family

    Children are welcome in the service, and the youth meet on Friday evenings.

Our mission

Faith worked out in practice, not in theory

We exist to help people overcome the difficulties life presents through a practical demonstration of how to live the Christian life. That happens through discipleship: prayer, fellowship and outreach, learned alongside people who are doing it already.

Our core experiences are salvation, water baptism and the baptism of the Holy Spirit. Everything else follows from there.
