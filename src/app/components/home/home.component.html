ABOUT US

A Community Built on Faith

At The Potters House Melville Church, we help people live the Christian life through practical lessons and leading by example. We believe in guiding others through faith, prayer, fellowship, and outreach, showing God’s love in action. Our core experiences include salvation, water baptism, and Holy Spirit baptism, and we seek to reflect Christ in everything we do.
